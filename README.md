# Anatolian Beyliks - Civilization V DLC

## Overview
Anatolian Beyliks is a comprehensive mod for Civilization V that introduces the **Osmanogullari** civilization, one of the most historically significant Anatolian principalities. This DLC allows you to lead Osman Bey and guide the Osmanoğulları Beyliği from a small territorial beylik to an emerging empire.

## Civilization Features

### Leader: Osman Bey
**Subtitle:** Bey of Osmanogullari  
**Era:** 1533 AD - 1584 AD

Osman Bey was the founder of the Osmanoğulları Beyliği, a Turkic principality that would eventually become one of history's greatest empires. He was a visionary leader with balanced diplomatic acumen, military prowess, and an affinity for naval expansion.

#### Leader Traits
- **Boldness:** 4/10 - Measured in military aggression
- **Diplomacy:** 6/10 - Skilled at diplomatic relations
- **Warmonger Hate:** 6/10 - Moderate disapproval of warmongering
- **Loyalty:** 7/10 - Steadfast in commitments
- **Forgiveness:** 6/10 - Reasonable in accepting apologies

#### AI Approach
Osman Bey tends toward **guarded diplomatic stances** with a notable preference for:
- Naval dominance (FLAVOR_NAVAL: 8/10)
- Water connection development (FLAVOR_WATER_CONNECTION: 8/10)
- City defense and infrastructure (FLAVOR_CITY_DEFENSE: 7/10)
- Gold and culture generation (FLAVOR_GOLD: 7/10, FLAVOR_CULTURE: 6/10)
- Minor civilization alliances (MINOR_CIV_APPROACH_FRIENDLY & PROTECTIVE: 7/10)

## Unique Abilities

### Trait: Spirit of Ergenekon
*"Expansion of the Kayı Tribe"*

The defining ability of the Osmanoğulları, representing the legendary exodus and revival of the Turkic Kayı tribe.

**Abilities:**
- Construct **Kasaba** and **Hisar** improvements
- Receive **+2 Gold** and **+1 Culture** per Kasaba

---

## Unique Units & Structures

### Unique Building: Kiler
**Prerequisite:** Pottery  
**Cost:** 60 Production  
**Maintenance:** 1 Gold

An improved granary unique to the Anatolian Beyliks, the Kiler represents the sophisticated storage systems of Ottoman settlements.

**Benefits:**
- **+3 Food** and **+2 Production** and **+1 Gold** per worked tile
- **+1 Production** from each Forest and Jungle tile
- **+1 Trade Route** slot
- Enhanced yields on all terrain types (Grassland, Plains, Desert, Tundra)

---

## Unique Improvements

### Kasaba
**Prerequisite:** Pottery  
**Construction Time:** 600 production turns  
**Available Terrain:** Grassland, Plains, Desert, Tundra, Snow

A thriving settlement improvement reflecting the marketplaces and trade hubs of Anatolia.

**Yields:**
- **+3 Food** - Reflects agricultural surplus
- **+3 Production** - Bustling marketplace activity
- **+2 Gold** - Trade and commerce
- **+2 Culture** - Cultural exchange
- **+1 Faith** - Religious significance
- **+1 Science** - Knowledge centers

**Unique Bonuses:**
- +2 Gold and +1 Culture per Kasaba (from Spirit of Ergenekon trait)

### Hisar (Fortress)
**Prerequisite:** Mining  
**Construction Time:** 600 production turns  
**Available Terrain:** Grassland, Plains, Desert, Tundra, Snow  
**Special:** Can be built outside your borders

A strategic fortification representing the defensive hisars of the Anatolian frontier.

**Yields:**
- **+5 Production** - Military fortress activity

**Combat Benefits:**
- **+160% Defense Modifier** - Significant defensive advantage
- **+50 Nearby Enemy Damage** - Damages adjacent enemy units
- **+1 Culture Bomb Radius** - Claims 6-tile range territory
- **250 Gold** upon pillaging

## Gameplay Characteristics

### Strengths
✓ **Naval Dominance** - Exceptional preference for naval units and water-based strategies  
✓ **Defensive Builder** - Strong city defense capabilities and fortification construction  
✓ **Economic Growth** - Consistent gold and food generation through improvements  
✓ **Cultural Development** - Moderate culture generation for ideology advancement  
✓ **Diplomatic Flexibility** - Can pursue various diplomatic approaches effectively  

### Strategy Tips
- **Naval Focus:** Prioritize naval units and water connections for maximum bonuses
- **Settlement Expansion:** Kasabas are highly efficient improvements; spread them across your empire
- **Frontier Fortification:** Use Hisars to claim and defend territory, especially on borders
- **Trade Routes:** The Kiler's bonus trade route slot enables robust economic networks
- **Minor Civs:** Osman Bey favors friendly relations with city-states for consistent allies

## Historical Context
The Anatolian Beyliks were small Turkish principalities that emerged in Anatolia following the decline of the Seljuk Empire. The Osmanoğulları Beyliği, founded by Osman Bey, was among the most successful, eventually expanding to become the Ottoman Empire—one of history's longest-lasting and most influential civilizations. This mod celebrates that remarkable rise from modest principality to imperial power.
